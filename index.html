a friendly semi-vanilla world for people who just want to build, explore, meet new people and occasionally pvp. No economy, just a world worth taking care of

Main Commands:

-------------------------------

• /pvp - toggles your pvp

• /tpa - request teleport to another player

• /sethome - set a home

• /home - teleport to a home

• /spawn - teleport to spawn

• /playtime - check your playtime

• /ranks - shows the playtime rank progression
